Delete your DiveMind account

This page explains how to delete your DiveMind account and the progress backed up with it. An account is optional: you only have one if you signed in with Google or with an email and password to back up your progress.

Delete it in the app

1. Open DiveMind and go to Settings.

2. Tap Back up your progress.

3. Tap Delete account, then confirm. If you signed in with an email and password, you are asked for your password first.

Your account and its backup are deleted right away. The progress on this phone stays, and you can keep playing without an account.

Delete it without the app

Write to divemind.help@gmail.com from the email address of your account and ask us to delete it. We delete the account and its backup within 30 days and write back when it is done.

What is deleted

Your account: your email address and how you sign in (Firebase Authentication).

Your backup: the copy of your game results, progress, welcome answers, name and picture, and settings saved in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). Nothing of it is kept.

What is not deleted

The progress on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Usage statistics. They carry a random app ID, never your name, your email or your account, so they cannot be found from your account. Google Firebase Analytics deletes them after at most 14 months. You can stop them in Settings → Share usage stats.
