DiveMind privacy policy

Effective 13 November 2026

DiveMind is a set of short brain games. This policy explains what the app keeps and where it goes. In short: there is no account, no advertising and no analytics, and everything the app keeps stays on your phone. We do not receive it.

What the app keeps on your phone

Your game results: which game, when, how long, the level and how it went.

Your progress: levels, points, streak, achievements, your goal and the daily dive.

The answers you give in the welcome questions, such as what you want to work on.

Your settings: language, sounds, vibration, reminder time, and which tips and game demos you have already seen.

What leaves your phone

Nothing is sent to us. The app has no servers, no analytics and no advertising, and it does not need an internet connection.

Sharing: when you tap Share, the text or image goes to the app you choose, and that app handles it under its own policy.

Phone backups: if your phone backs up its apps (for example Google or iCloud backup), DiveMind's data may be included. Google and Apple handle those backups under their own policies.

Reminders

Daily and weekly reminders are scheduled on your phone. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone's settings.

Deleting your data

Reports → Your data deletes your game history, insights and reports. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it kept.

Children

DiveMind does not collect personal information from anyone, including children: nothing the app keeps leaves the phone.

Your scores

Points, levels and charts only measure how you play DiveMind's games, inside the app. They are not a test of intelligence, memory or health, and DiveMind is not a medical product.

Changes

If DiveMind ever starts sending data anywhere (for example to count how the app is used, or to keep an account), this policy will be updated first and the app will tell you before it starts.
