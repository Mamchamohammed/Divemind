DiveMind privacy policy

Effective 18 November 2026

DiveMind is a set of short brain games. This policy explains what the app keeps and where it goes. In short: there is no advertising; an account is optional and only backs up your progress; and the app counts how it is used, without your name or email, unless you turn that off or are under 13.

What the app keeps on your phone

Your game results: which game, when, how long, the level and how it went.

Your progress: levels, points, streak, achievements, your goal and the daily dive.

The answers you give in the welcome questions, such as what you want to work on and your age range (never your birth date).

Your settings: language, sounds, vibration, reminder time, and which tips and game demos you have already seen.

Your name and picture, if you set them: the name you type and which of the app's own pictures you picked. No photo is taken or uploaded.

Your account and backup (optional)

You can sign in with Google or with an email and password to back up your progress, so it is not lost if you uninstall the app or change phones. Players under 13 are never offered an account.

Signed in, the app saves a copy of everything listed above to Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), linked to your account, when you leave the app and when you tap Back up now. Firebase Authentication keeps your email address and how you sign in. Only you can read your backup. Sleep and mood check-in answers are not included: they stay on your phone.

Settings → Back up your progress → Delete account deletes your account and its backup. The progress on your phone stays.

Usage statistics

To see which parts of DiveMind help people and which do not, the app sends Google Firebase Analytics events such as which screens are opened, which games are finished and with what result, whether the daily dive was completed, and whether a goal or a backup was set up. They come with a random app ID and general device information, never your name, your email or an advertising ID.

You can turn this off in Settings → Share usage stats. It is always off for players under 13.

What else leaves your phone

Nothing else. The app has no advertising, and the games work without an internet connection.

Sharing: when you tap Share, the text or image goes to the app you choose, and that app handles it under its own policy.

Phone backups: if your phone backs up its apps (for example Google or iCloud backup), DiveMind's data may be included. Google and Apple handle those backups under their own policies.

Reminders

Daily and weekly reminders are scheduled on your phone. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone's settings.

Deleting your data

Reports → Your data deletes your game history, insights and reports. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it kept on the phone; a backup in your account stays until you delete the account.

Children

Players who tell us they are under 13 are never offered an account, and no usage statistics are sent for them: nothing the app keeps for them leaves the phone.

Your scores

Points, levels and charts only measure how you play DiveMind's games, inside the app. They are not a test of intelligence, memory or health, and DiveMind is not a medical product.

Changes

If DiveMind starts sending any other data, this policy will be updated first and the app will tell you before it starts.
